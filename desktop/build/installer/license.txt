LINE RUSH: THE SHORT VERSION

Line Rush is free to play, and its code is open source.

  * Play it as much as you like, on as many computers as you like.
  * Your scores and settings are saved on this computer, and in your
    Line Rush account if you make one.
  * Line Rush keeps itself up to date: it checks for new versions on
    GitHub and installs them for you.
  * Songs you import stay on this computer. They are never uploaded.

The rest of this page is the fine print that makes all of that official.
